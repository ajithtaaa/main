In 2017, Elon Musk promised a car that could fly. Everyone laughed.
Nine years later... he actually did it. Kind of.
It started because his friend Peter Thiel complained the future was supposed to have flying cars. So Elon basically said: fine. I'll make you one.
Which is funny, because Elon used to say flying cars were terrifying... because a loose hubcap could fall off and slice you in half.
So how does it fly? No wings. No propellers. Just ten nozzles blasting ultra high-pressure air... like a rocket strapped to a sports car.
On demo day, at a SpaceX rocket site in Texas, the car shot off a steel ramp... did a full barrel roll in midair... and landed on its wheels.
Oh, and nobody was inside. Elon said that was so they didn't kill anyone.
It goes zero to sixty in under one second. Elon admitted it's not exactly a safety-optimized car... and might not even be legal everywhere.
The crowd kept yelling, "How much?" Nobody answered.
Production starts in 2028. So if you want a flying car... start saving now. And maybe check your hubcaps.
